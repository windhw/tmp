# Overseas (non-China) professions that use 3D DCC software at high frequency — roles, tools, workforce, pay, demand, and 2023–2026 disruption (US / Canada / UK-Europe / Japan / South Korea / India)

> Research date: 2026-10-11. Most data is from 2023–2026; older data is labeled by year. Every factual claim has an inline source. Role-to-software mappings that rest partly on standard industry practice rather than a fetched source are marked "[synthesis]" in the Inferences sections.
> Research limit: the web-search budget ran out partway through, so some planned lookups (India and Korea salaries, Fiverr and TurboSquid data, AMI medical-illustrator survey, Hitmarker job reports) are listed under Gaps.

## Q1. Which job roles use 3D DCC software daily as their primary tool, what do they do with it, and which software do they rely on? (tiered by intensity)

### Takeaway
The roles where 3D DCC is the main daily tool are the production-pipeline roles in games, film/TV VFX, animation, advertising/motion design, and visualization: modeler/3D artist, character, environment, prop/hard-surface, texture/look-dev, rigger/TA, 3D animator, FX TD, lighting, layout/previs, motion designer (3D), archviz, product CGI, and real-time/Unreal roles. The best available industry data (ArtStation 2026, GDC 2026, School of Motion, CGarchitect, CG Spectrum) points to a core stack: Maya, Blender, ZBrush, Substance 3D Painter, Unreal, Unity, Houdini, Cinema 4D and 3ds Max (with V-Ray/Corona), alongside Photoshop. Which package dominates depends on the sector.

### Cited Findings
**What the core occupation does (US official definition)**
- BLS "Special Effects Artists and Animators" (SOC 27-1014) "create two- and three-dimensional models, images that appear to move, and visual effects for television, movies, video games, and other media". Duties include using computer programs to create graphics and animation and editing work based on feedback. For CGI they may animate 3D characters from recorded actor movements. BLS adds: "Many animation companies have their own computer animation software that artists must learn to use." — [BLS OOH](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/arts-and-design/multimedia-artists-and-animators.htm)

**Software most used by artists and most requested by employers (2025 data)**
- ArtStation *State of the Art Report 2026* (covers 2025; >2,000 artists surveyed plus a studio/recruiter survey):
  - Most popular software among artists, in order: Photoshop, Blender, Substance 3D Painter, Maya, ZBrush.
  - Most-required software in job listings, in order: Photoshop, Unreal Engine, Blender, Maya, Unity.
  - Common pairings: Maya + Substance 3D Painter; Substance 3D Painter + ZBrush; Blender + Photoshop.
  - 3D modeling is the top portfolio skill. 3D animation grew 14.4%.
  - Recruiters say "3D and concept art are needed the most". Hiring by industry, in order: Games, then Animation, Film & TV, Asset Creation, Advertising, AR/VR.
  - No percentages were published. — [80.lv summary of ArtStation report, 10 Apr 2026](https://80.lv/articles/learn-what-recruiters-want-from-artists-in-artstation-s-report)
- Game engines (GDC 2026 State of the Game Industry, >2,300 respondents):
  - Unreal Engine is the primary engine for 42% of developers and Unity for 30%.
  - Unreal is primary for 59% at AA studios and 47% at AAA studios.
  - 54% of older indie studios still use Unity. Godot is used by 11% of newer indies. — [GDC 2026 report article](https://gdconf.com/article/gdc-2026-state-of-the-game-industry-reveals-impact-of-layoffs-generative-ai-and-more/)

**Game-art specializations and their software**
- CG Spectrum's game-artist career page maps specializations to software:
  - Character: Maya, 3ds Max, ZBrush, MotionBuilder.
  - Modeling: Maya, 3ds Max, ZBrush, Blender.
  - Animation: Maya, MotionBuilder.
  - Engines: Unreal Engine, Unity.
  - Other specializations listed: lighting/shading, texturing, and special effects (explosions, smoke, weather, particles).
  - It notes game artists are often generalist animators or modelers. US game-artist pay is quoted at about $69,000 (junior) to $124,000 (senior), citing Glassdoor. — [CG Spectrum](https://www.cgspectrum.com/career-pathways/game-artist)

**Motion design**
- School of Motion (Feb 2026 salary guide):
  - "Cinema 4D, Blender, and Houdini are the key tools."
  - "3D proficiency is one of the single strongest salary multipliers in motion design."
  - Unreal Engine is "a genuinely underserved niche" in entertainment and automotive. — [School of Motion](https://schoolofmotion.com/blog/motion-design-salaries-in-2026-your-complete-guide-to-what-you-can-earn-and-how-to-earn-more)
- An unverified third-party stats page claims 62% Cinema 4D usage among motion designers in 2023 and 40% year-on-year Blender growth. It names no source, so it is not reliable. — [Gitnux](https://gitnux.org/motion-design-industry-statistics/)

**Archviz (architectural visualization)**
- CGarchitect 2020 rendering survey (2,529 responses): V-Ray and Corona are the most widely used renderers in architectural production. Use of Unreal Engine, Twinmotion and Enscape "has risen significantly". This is 2020 data. — [CG Channel on CGarchitect 2020 survey](https://www.cgchannel.com/2020/09/cgarchitect-2020-rendering-survey-reveals-trends-in-arch-viz)
- Archviz job postings lean on 3ds Max, SketchUp, Revit or Blender. This comes from job-board aggregation, not a systematic count. — [ZipRecruiter / search aggregation](https://www.ziprecruiter.com/Jobs/3D-Artist)

**Virtual production**
- The Unreal Engine operator is a distinct, currently hired role:
  - Pixomondo advertised Unreal Engine operator roles for an LED stage in Riyadh (Apr–May 2026). It also advertised an on-set "Engine Operator", described as the primary Unreal operator during production. — [EntertainmentCareers – Pixomondo UE operator](https://www.entertainmentcareers.net/pixomondo/unreal-engine-operator/job/524028/); [Engine Operator](https://www.entertainmentcareers.net/pixomondo/engine-operator/job/445337)
  - Frame.io (2022) called the volume operator role new, often requiring training from scratch. — [Frame.io](https://blog.frame.io/2022/02/07/virtual-production-jobs-roles-careers/)

**Medical / scientific illustration**
- 3D work uses Autodesk Maya, Cinema 4D and ZBrush. The source is a low-quality guide, not the AMI. — [websitebuilderinsider](https://www.websitebuilderinsider.com/how-much-does-a-medical-illustrator-make-a-year/)

**Japan anime: CG is a large, mostly employed job category**
- The Association of Japanese Animations (AJA) estimates about 2,000 "CG" workers out of about 18,000 animation production staff (text says ~20,000). CG staff are "Mostly Employed" and paid "per cut/primarily person-hour". By contrast, key animators (~1,000) and in-betweeners (~3,000) are "Mostly Freelance". — [AJA, "Current Status on the Labor Conditions of Animation Creators in Japan" (PDF, 2024)](https://aja.gr.jp/info/2521)

**Blender professionals**
- Blender's 2024 user survey (>7,000 respondents):
  - "More than half" use Blender daily or weekly.
  - 44% make money with Blender, and about 1/5 use it as a hobby.
  - Among professionals, film & animation is largest, followed by graphic design and game development.
  - Top respondent countries: US, India, Germany. — [Blender Survey 2024 results](https://survey.blender.org/feedback/2024/); [80.lv](https://80.lv/articles/blender-s-feedback-survey-2024-results-are-here)
- Poliigon's 2024 *State of 3D* survey (6,251 respondents, skewed toward hobbyist generalists): 87% of 3ds Max and Cinema 4D users use the software professionally, versus about 50% of Blender, Unity and Unreal users. This is from a search snippet; the page returned 403 when fetched. — [Poliigon State of 3D 2024](https://poliigon.com/articles/state-of-3d-2024)

- Studio pipelines differ by sector [synthesis]:
  - Film/TV VFX and feature animation are built around Maya, Houdini, Katana and Nuke, often with proprietary tools (BLS notes the latter†).
  - AAA games use Maya or 3ds Max, ZBrush, Substance, and Unreal or proprietary engines.
  - Indie games, freelance, advertising and small studios lean toward Blender.
  - Motion graphics leans toward Cinema 4D, with Blender rising.
  - Archviz relies on 3ds Max with V-Ray or Corona, moving toward real-time Unreal, Twinmotion and D5.
- The Poliigon split (87% of 3ds Max/C4D users professional vs ~50% for Blender/Unity/Unreal) suggests [synthesis]:
  - Paid "legacy" DCCs sit almost entirely with professionals.
  - Blender's user base is much larger but includes many hobbyists and students.
  - So Blender's share of artists overstates its share of studio seats.

### Gaps
- No public, systematic 2024–2026 count of software mentions across 3D job postings exists in the sources I could reach. ArtStation gives ranks only, with no percentages. A real count would need a LinkedIn/Indeed scrape.
- No authoritative time-use data (hours per day in DCC) by role. Tiering is by role definition, not measured hours.
- Houdini's share of FX jobs, Katana's share of lighting jobs, and Nuke's share of compositing jobs are standard practice, but I found no fetched quantitative source in this session.

## Q2. Which roles use 3D tools only occasionally or as secondary tools?

### Takeaway
Large adjacent occupations use 3D occasionally or for specific tasks, usually as an add-on to CAD, 2D design or web work. They include graphic designers, industrial/product designers, architects, art directors, UI/UX and web designers, marketing/e-commerce content teams, and concept artists. In the US they far outnumber the core 3D occupation (e.g., ~198k graphic designers vs ~20k wage-and-salary special-effects artists/animators). They are a big secondary market for Blender, C4D, KeyShot, Substance and Adobe 3D tools, but 3D is not their daily primary tool.

### Cited Findings

|---|---|---|---|

| Commercial & Industrial Designers (27-1021) | 33,490 | $83,910 | Real wage change 2015–2025: −8.0% |
| Art Directors (27-1011) | 53,070 | $114,850 | |
| Web & Digital Interface Designers (15-1255) | 113,330 | $104,000 | |
| Special Effects Artists & Animators (27-1014), for comparison | 19,970 (wage-and-salary only) | $102,030 | Real wage +17.4% (2015–2025) |

— [Adobe, "Economic State of Creative Professions", June 2026 (BLS OEWS May 2025)](https://www.adobe.com/ai/research/202606/economic-state-of-creative-professions.html)

**Industry signals**
- Recruiters list Advertising and AR/VR as hiring sectors, behind Games, Animation, Film & TV and Asset Creation. — [80.lv / ArtStation 2026](https://80.lv/articles/learn-what-recruiters-want-from-artists-in-artstation-s-report)
- Among Blender professionals, graphic design is the second-largest field after film/animation. Graphic designers are therefore a notable Blender user group, likely adding 3D elements to 2D work. — [Blender Survey 2024](https://survey.blender.org/feedback/2024/)
- Poliigon's 2025 State of 3D survey (3,779 responses) reports advertising as the dominant use of 3D work. — [80.lv on Poliigon State of 3D 2025, 12 Nov 2025](https://80.lv/articles/despite-the-hype-only-22-of-3d-artists-regularly-use-ai)

### Inferences
**Occasional / secondary users [synthesis]**
- **Architects:** model mainly in Revit, ArchiCAD, Rhino or SketchUp. They use real-time viewers (Enscape, Twinmotion, D5, Lumion) for presentation. Heavy rendering is often outsourced to archviz specialists (Tier 1 above).
- **Industrial/product designers:** work in SolidWorks, Fusion 360, Rhino or Creo. They render in KeyShot. Blender or C4D appears only for marketing visuals.
- **Graphic designers / brand designers:** occasional 3D for type, mock-ups and packaging (Adobe Dimension/Substance 3D Stager, Blender, C4D, Spline).
- **UI/UX and web designers:** 3D elements via Spline, Three.js, Blender exports.
- **Marketing and e-commerce teams:** commission or template product renders; increasingly use AI or configurators.
- **Concept artists:** use Blender or ZBrush for 3D block-outs under paintovers. This is regular but secondary to Photoshop, which ArtStation still ranks as #1 software overall.
- **Engineers and CAD drafters:** mechanical CAD is a separate tool family, not DCC.

**Size and trend implications [synthesis]**
- The occasional-user population is roughly an order of magnitude larger than the core 3D occupation in the US.
- Graphic designers fell 7.7% in one year (May 2024 to May 2025, BLS via Adobe). This adjacent pool is under pressure, plausibly linked to AI and economic weakness, but the source draws no causal link.

### Gaps
- No BLS May 2025 figure for architects (SOC 17-1011) was retrieved in this session.
- No survey measured how often architects, industrial designers or marketers open DCC tools. The tiering here is qualitative.

## Q3. Workforce size and salary data (US, Canada, UK/Europe, Japan, Korea, India)

### Takeaway
- **US:** the core 3D occupation is small but well paid: BLS counts 51,900 jobs in 2025 (63% self-employed), with a $102,030 median (May 2025) and 0% projected growth for 2025–35.
- **Japan:** anime CG staff are about 2,000 people. Animation pay averages 4.555M yen, and the small 3DCG sample earns less (median 2.52M yen).
- **India:** about 260,000 AVGC professionals, the largest pool.
- **Korea:** 87,576 people work in the game industry.
- **UK:** the screen-crew workforce is 244,500–252,000 but was running at only 50–55% capacity in 2025.
- **Salary data quality:** salaries for specialized roles (TA, FX TD, Houdini) sit at the top end. Most role-level salary data outside the US comes from aggregators of uneven quality.

### Cited Findings
**United States**
- BLS OOH, Special Effects Artists and Animators (27-1014):
  - Median pay $102,030/yr ($49.06/hr), May 2025. Lowest 10% earn <$58,600; highest 10% earn >$177,110.
  - 51,900 jobs in 2025. Outlook 0% (−100 jobs) for 2025–35, with about 4,200 openings/yr.
  - Employment share: self-employed 63%; motion picture & video 10%; software publishers 7%; computer systems design 4%; advertising/PR 2%.
  - Median by industry: software publishers $127,670; computer systems design $103,390; motion picture & video $100,990; advertising/PR $86,290.
  - BLS notes that AI "can complete some animation and effects tasks faster, which may reduce demand." — [BLS OOH](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/arts-and-design/multimedia-artists-and-animators.htm)
- The previous BLS projection cycle (2024–34) used a 2024 base of 57,100, projected to 58,000 by 2034 (+1.6%, ~5,000 openings/yr).
  - The 2025 base (51,900) is about 9% lower.
  - Mean wage May 2025: $112,870. Nominal median rose +59.5% from 2015 ($63,970) to 2025 ($102,030), +17.4% real.
  - OEWS wage-and-salary employment (excluding self-employed): 19,970. — [Adobe/BLS compilation](https://www.adobe.com/ai/research/202606/economic-state-of-creative-professions.html)
  - The May 2024 median was $99,800. — [School of Motion citing BLS](https://schoolofmotion.com/blog/motion-design-salaries-in-2026-your-complete-guide-to-what-you-can-earn-and-how-to-earn-more)
- Animation Guild (IATSE Local 839) contract minimum: journey-level Animator $2,255.20/week ($56.38/hr) for the period ending 7/31/24, about $117k/yr if annualized. This is a contract minimum, not a survey median. — [TAG 2021–24 wage scale PDF](https://animationguild.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/10/2021-24-Local-839-WAGES-v02.pdf)
- Game artists (US):
  - About $69k junior to $124k senior (Glassdoor via CG Spectrum). — [CG Spectrum](https://www.cgspectrum.com/career-pathways/game-artist)
  - Aggregator estimates: technical artist about $108k and environment artist about $88k median. Payscale lists environment artists at about $50k, which conflicts. Low-quality aggregators. — [gamedesignskills.com](https://gamedesignskills.com/game-development/game-environment-artist-vs-designer-salary/); [Payscale](https://www.payscale.com/research/US/Job=Environment_Artist/Salary/3dfa2007/Games)

  - 3D motion designers earn a premium. Germany (ERI): €66,903 for 3D vs €42,800–€48,000 for standard motion graphics (about 40–55% premium). London: 3D motion designer £69,021. — [School of Motion](https://schoolofmotion.com/blog/motion-design-salaries-in-2026-your-complete-guide-to-what-you-can-earn-and-how-to-earn-more)

Not reliable. — [koha-community mirror page](https://ratings.koha-community.org/en/medical-illustrator-salary.html); [Vault](https://vault.com/professions/medical-illustrators-and-photographers)

## Q4. Job-posting trends: which software is most requested (Maya vs Blender vs Houdini vs Unreal) and how Blender demand has grown

### Takeaway
In 2025–2026 employer job listings, Unreal Engine ranks just behind Photoshop, ahead of Blender, Maya and Unity (ArtStation 2026). Blender has moved from hobbyist tool to a requested studio skill: it is now #2 among artists, #3 in listings, and in January 2026 Netflix Animation Studios became a top-tier funder. Maya still anchors film/VFX character animation and rigging, and Houdini is a high-value niche for FX and procedural work. Real-time (Unreal) skills carry the strongest growth story, but evidence is mostly vendor-commissioned or rank-only.

### Cited Findings
**Current software rankings and engine share**
- ArtStation 2026:
  - Listing ranks: Photoshop > Unreal Engine > Blender > Maya > Unity.
  - Artist usage ranks: Photoshop > Blender > Substance 3D Painter > Maya > ZBrush.
  - Reasons artists switched software: price changes 50%, better alternatives 24%, licensing changes 22%, ethical/business concerns 17%. — [80.lv](https://80.lv/articles/learn-what-recruiters-want-from-artists-in-artstation-s-report)
- GDC 2026: Unreal is the primary engine for 42% vs Unity 30%. Unreal is primary for 59% of AA and 47% of AAA developers. — [GDC](https://gdconf.com/article/gdc-2026-state-of-the-game-industry-reveals-impact-of-layoffs-generative-ai-and-more/)

**Real-time 3D demand (older, vendor-commissioned)**
- Burning Glass study commissioned by Epic, 2019 (dated):
  - 315,246 US job postings called for 3D graphics skills in 2018, more than all data-science postings.
  - Unreal Engine skills were the fastest-growing real-time 3D segment, with the highest salary premiums.
  - UK demand for Unreal skills quadrupled since 2013; Canada saw a fivefold increase.
  - Real-time 3D demand grew 601% faster than the overall job market. Burning Glass forecast 138% Unreal growth globally over 10 years. — [Unreal Engine blog, 29 May 2019](https://www.unrealengine.com/blog/demand-for-unreal-engine-and-real-time-3d-skills-at-all-time-high); [follow-up report](https://www.unrealengine.com/blog/new-report-shows-high-demand-for-real-time-3d-skills-in-global-job-market)

**Blender's institutional adoption**
- Netflix Animation Studios joined the Blender Development Fund as Corporate Patron (top tier) in January 2026, for at least €240,000/yr, about four full-time developers.
  - Other top-tier backers: Epic Games, Bolt Graphics, Pico, Wacom.
  - Blender CEO Francesco Siddi called it recognition that Blender is "becoming more deeply integrated into the workflows of high-end animation studios". — [CG Channel, 28 Jan 2026](https://www.cgchannel.com/2026/01/netflix-donates-e240000year-to-the-blender-development-fund/); [Cartoon Brew](https://www.cartoonbrew.com/tools/netflix-animation-blender-development-fund-258516.html)
- Blender Foundation income rose to €3.1M in 2024 from €2.55M in 2023; donations rose almost 20%. — [BlenderNation](https://www.blendernation.com/2025/09/16/blender-foundation-annual-report-2024/); [CGPress](https://cgpress.org/archives/blender-foundation-reports-higher-donations-but-warns-of-low-reserves-in-2024-annual-report.html)
- Blender downloads: blender.org reports about 430,000 downloads/month from its download page in a non-release month (undated page). A figure of about 20 million downloads in 2023 appeared in search results, but its original page was not verified. — [blender.org](https://www.blender.org/media-exposure/almost-half-a-million-downloads-per-month/)

**Maya vs Blender in postings (qualitative only)**
- Industry blogs consistently say:
  - Mid-to-large studio animation and rigging postings list Maya as a requirement.
  - Blender appears more in indie, game-asset and generalist listings.
  - Many game-artist postings now say "Maya or Blender".
  - These are blog and vendor sources, not counts. — [Stepico](https://stepico.com/blog/maya-vs-blender-which-3d-software-is-right-for-you/); [SuperRenders](https://superrendersfarm.com/article/blender-vs-maya-comparison-2026)
- Indian training academies report that Bangalore feature studios "almost exclusively" require Maya for character animation and creature rigging. — [MAAC Jayanagar](https://www.maacjayanagar.com/blog/maya-vs-blender-2026)

**Unreal job ads in the UK (mostly programming)**
- ITJobsWatch: 83 ads (6 months to Oct 2024) → 12 (2025) → 40 (2026). The 2025 trough mirrors games layoffs. — [ITJobsWatch](https://www.itjobswatch.co.uk/jobs/uk/unreal%20engine.do)

### Inferences
- **Software hierarchy by sector, 2026 [synthesis from cited ranks plus industry practice]:**
  - **Games:** Unreal (engine) + Maya/Blender (DCC) + ZBrush + Substance Painter. Blender's share is rising fastest, especially at indie and mid-size studios.
  - **Film/feature animation:** Maya (animation/rigging) + Houdini (FX) + Katana/Nuke, with Blender entering. Netflix's funding is the strongest signal yet.
  - **Motion/ads:** Cinema 4D + Blender + Houdini.
  - **Archviz:** 3ds Max + V-Ray/Corona, shifting to Unreal/Twinmotion/D5.
- **Blender's growth drivers [synthesis]:** cost and licensing are the main forces. 50% of ArtStation switchers cite price changes and 22% licensing changes, which reflects Autodesk subscription pricing. Blender's share of studio seats likely lags its share of artists, because only about half of Blender users are professional (Poliigon 2024).
- **Unreal's position [synthesis]:** Unreal is the most requested 3D-specific product in listings. Demand is cyclical: UK ad counts fell sharply in 2025, then partly recovered in 2026.

### Gaps
- No independent 2023–2026 quantitative job-posting analysis (e.g., Lightcast/LinkedIn) with percentage shares for Maya, Blender, Houdini and Unreal was found.
- No Houdini-specific job-demand statistics were found.
- Blender's official 2025 "by the numbers" figures (downloads, users) were not located.
- Not verified this session, but worth checking: *Flow* (2024, Latvia), a Blender-made film, was reportedly the 2025 Oscar winner for Best Animated Feature. The report writer should verify before citing.

## Q5. Freelance / gig economy and asset marketplaces

### Takeaway
A large share of 3D work is freelance: 63% of the US core occupation is self-employed, and "inconsistent income" is artists' #1 complaint (57%). Asset marketplaces are still growing: CGTrader designer payouts hit a record (+13% in 2025) and listings grew 18.5% in the year to May 2026. AI-generated uploads are flooding supply (1 in 6 uploads) but capture almost no revenue (about 1.1%). Platform-level 3D demand data from Upwork/Fiverr is not public in a usable form.

### Cited Findings
**Self-employment and income instability**
- 63% of US Special Effects Artists and Animators are self-employed. — [BLS OOH](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/arts-and-design/multimedia-artists-and-animators.htm)
- ArtStation 2026: inconsistent income (57%) is the top artist challenge. — [80.lv](https://80.lv/articles/learn-what-recruiters-want-from-artists-in-artstation-s-report)

**CGTrader marketplace**
- Scale: more than 2 million professional 3D models and over 11 million community users. Designer payouts reached a record in 2025, growing 13%. — [CGTrader news](https://cgtrader.com/blog/news?page=15); [CGTrader LinkedIn](https://linkedin.com/company/cgtrader)
- CGTrader 2026 market trends report (June 2025–May 2026):
  - Listings grew 18.5%; sell-through rate 18.4%.
  - About 1 in 6 uploaded models is AI-generated, but AI assets account for only $1 of every $90 in revenue (about 1.1%).
  - Of buyers who bought AI assets: 20% found them not good enough, 7% used them only with heavy editing, 5% said they worked well. Among 3D-printing buyers, 4% said AI "works well".
  - Quality ranks above price as a purchase factor.
  - CEO Dalia Lašaitė: "AI uploads are currently growing faster than AI purchases."
  - CGTrader is partnering with Tencent on "AI-accelerated" topology, texture and variation workflows. — [404 Media, 12 Aug 2026](https://404media.co/ai-generated-3d-models-flood-market-but-almost-no-one-is-buying-them); [Fabbaloo (search snippet)](https://www.fabbaloo.com/?p=238959)

**Creator revenue streams (Blender survey 2024)**
- 44% of respondents make money with Blender. About 1/6 of those who earn from art also earn from selling assets, and about 1/20 from add-on development (the Blender Market / Superhive ecosystem). — [Blender Survey 2024](https://survey.blender.org/feedback/2024/)

**Freelance rates and platforms**
- Motion-design freelance day rates run $350–$2,000+/day by seniority and niche, with Houdini and AR/VR at the top. — [School of Motion](https://schoolofmotion.com/blog/motion-design-salaries-in-2026-your-complete-guide-to-what-you-can-earn-and-how-to-earn-more)
- Upwork's 2025 in-demand skills report highlights generative-AI modeling and data annotation. 49% of businesses use freelancers to fill skill gaps. No 3D-specific figures were published in the materials found. — [Upwork press release, 15 Jan 2025](https://www.upwork.com/press/releases/upwork-unveils-2025s-most-in-demand-skills)

**AI 3D generators (vendor claim)**
- Meshy claims more than 10 million users and 100 million+ models generated as of GDC 2026. Its free-tier output is CC BY 4.0, which limits commercial use. — [Cinevva guide](https://app.cinevva.com/guides/ai-3d-model-generators); [levelup.gitconnected](https://levelup.gitconnected.com/building-game-ready-3d-assets-fast-the-best-ai-generators-for-production-workflows-in-2026-f1f725ff5df0)

### Inferences
- **Two-tier freelance economy [synthesis]:**
  - Upper tier: senior and specialist freelancers (Houdini, Unreal, 3D motion), commanding $800–$2,000/day.
  - Lower tier: a large global mass of marketplace and gig modelers, where AI-generated supply is raising competition for discoverability.
- **AI and marketplace revenue [synthesis].** So far, buyers are not substituting AI assets for human-made ones at scale (1.1% of revenue). Commodity, low-end modeling gigs (simple props, 3D-print figures) are the most likely to erode as quality improves.
- **Content creators [synthesis].** Tutorial YouTubers, add-on developers and asset sellers form a real income layer around Blender (about 1/6 of earners sell assets; about 1/20 sell add-ons). It is smaller than studio employment and concentrated around Blender rather than paid DCCs.

## Q6. Impact of 2023–2026 industry contraction (game/VFX layoffs, Hollywood strikes) and of generative AI on these roles

### Takeaway
From 2023 to 2025 the overseas 3D labor market went through its sharpest contraction in a decade.
- **Games:** about 10.5k layoffs in 2023 and 14.6k–16.8k in 2024. 28% of developers were laid off within two years (33% in the US).
- **Film/TV VFX:** post-strike volume declines, and Technicolor (MPC, The Mill) collapsed in February 2025.
- **UK screen crews** ran at 50–55% capacity in 2025.
- **California's film/TV sector** is about 25% smaller than in 2022.

Generative AI is not yet a measured cause of job losses. It is a major anticipated threat, though:
- Visual/technical artists are the most negative group (64%).
- A 2024 industry study forecast ~21% of US film/TV/animation jobs affected by 2026, with 3D asset generation expected to be effective.

Actual AI adoption among 3D artists remains limited: 68% never use AI 3D generators.

### Cited Findings
**Game industry contraction**
- GDC 2026 State of the Game Industry (>2,300 respondents, released Jan 2026):
  - 28% laid off in the past two years (33% of US respondents). About half say their current or last employer had layoffs in the past 12 months.
  - Two-thirds of AAA respondents report company layoffs, vs one-third at indies. 74% of students are worried about job prospects.
  - US union support is 82%. — [GDC](https://gdconf.com/article/gdc-2026-state-of-the-game-industry-reveals-impact-of-layoffs-generative-ai-and-more/); [BusinessWire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260129438528/en/2026-State-of-the-Game-Industry-Report-Reveals-Widening-Effect-of-Layoffs-Broader-Perspectives-on-Generative-AI-Unionization-Tariffs-and-More)
- Games layoff trackers (confirmed cuts are floors):
  - 2022: 8,549; 2023: about 10,500 (10,466); 2024: 14,651, with an alternative count of 16,766.
  - 2025: about 6,709 by 22 July, incomplete. Totals for later 2025 conflict. — [Game World Observer](https://gameworldobserver.com/2024/06/03/over-10k-games-industry-layoffs-in-2024-so-far-vs-2023); [Udonis](https://www.blog.udonis.co/mobile-marketing/mobile-games/game-industry-layoffs); [Statista](https://www.statista.com/statistics/1458214/worldwide-gaming-industry-layoffs); [Yahoo/16,000 chart](https://malaysia.news.yahoo.com/impact-16-000-games-industry-212436480.html)

**Film/TV VFX contraction**
- Technicolor Group (parent of MPC, The Mill, Mikros Animation) began shutting down in February 2025:
  - It issued US WARN notices and entered a French court recovery procedure.
  - Its UK arm went into administration, with "the majority" of about 440 UK staff made redundant. The global workforce was estimated at about 4,500.
  - CG Channel called it potentially the most significant VFX job shake-up since Rhythm & Hues' bankruptcy. — [CG Channel, 25 Feb 2025](https://www.cgchannel.com/2025/02/technicolor-group-begins-to-shut-down-operations/); [AWN](https://awn.com/news/technicolor-mpc-mill-facing-full-shutdown); [Digital Production](https://digitalproduction.com/2025/02/24/technicolor-studios-on-the-brink-mpc-the-mill-mikros-animation-at-risk)
- Secondary reporting adds:
  - Technicolor UK headcount fell from 1,170 to 446 during 2024 (−62%).
  - About 2,000 Technicolor India staff went unpaid when payroll froze.
  - Deadline reported up to 10,000 people affected globally.
  - California's film/TV/sound sector is "approximately 25 percent smaller than in 2022" (Otis College 2025).
  - FilmLA Q2 2025: 5,394 shoot days, −6.2% year-on-year and >30% below the 5-year average. — [Stop Motion Magazine, 14 Oct 2025](https://stopmotionmagazine.com/where-did-all-the-jobs-go/)
- UK screen workforce: 50–55% of capacity in 2025 (vs 80–90% in 2022); content output −13% over 2022–2025. — [Televisual](https://www.televisual.com/news/screenskills-4skills-report-workforce-at-50-capacity/)
- The US core occupation's employment base fell from 57,100 (2024, BLS projections) to 51,900 (2025, BLS OOH). The 2025–35 outlook is 0%, and BLS explicitly cites AI as a possible demand reducer. — [BLS OOH](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/arts-and-design/multimedia-artists-and-animators.htm); [Adobe/BLS](https://www.adobe.com/ai/research/202606/economic-state-of-creative-professions.html)
- A moderator post on r/vfx (March 2025) describes 18–24 months of shrinking work volume and hiring. Anecdotal. — [r/vfx mirror](https://redlib.hackliberty.org/r/vfx)

**Generative AI: forecasts and sentiment**
- *Future Unscripted* study (CVL Economics for The Animation Guild, CAA and others; 300 executives surveyed Nov–Dec 2023):
  - About 21.4% of US film, TV and animation jobs (about 118,500) are likely to be consolidated, replaced or eliminated by GenAI by 2026. California accounts for 39,500 and New York 15,100.
  - 75% of respondents said GenAI had already supported job elimination, reduction or consolidation in their division.
  - 47% expected GenAI to be effective at generating 3D assets within 3 years. Only 26% said their workforce was fully prepared. — [TAG AI page](https://animationguild.org/ai-and-animation/)
- TAG's follow-up material names character/environment design, 3D modeling and animation/rigging as the most affected roles, with entry-level positions most at risk. A later guild study estimated about 29–30% of animation jobs could be disrupted within 3 years. These details come from search summaries, not the primary PDF. — [Animation Magazine](https://animationmagazine.net/?p=3257717); [TAG](https://animationguild.org/ai-and-animation/)
- TAG's 2024 contract requires studios to notify and consult before using AI, but does not let members opt out of using AI or having their work used for training. — [Sherwood News](https://www.sherwood.news/culture/the-animation-guild-ratified-its-new-contract-with-studios-despite-members)
- GDC 2026 sentiment and usage:
  - 52% say GenAI is harming the industry, up from 30% in 2025 and 18% in 2024. Only about 7% say it is positive.
  - Visual & technical art is the most negative discipline at 64%, vs 63% for design/narrative and 59% for programming.
  - 36% use GenAI at work (30% of studio employees), mostly for research/brainstorming (81%). — [GDC](https://gdconf.com/article/gdc-2026-state-of-the-game-industry-reveals-impact-of-layoffs-generative-ai-and-more/)

**Generative AI: actual adoption among 3D artists**
- Poliigon State of 3D 2025 (3,779 respondents):
  - Only 22% of 3D artists use AI regularly. 68% never use AI 3D model generators.
  - For AI image generators: 26% never use them, 15% weekly, 7% daily.
  - 46% of employers have no restrictions on AI use; 9% ban it. — [80.lv, 12 Nov 2025](https://80.lv/articles/despite-the-hype-only-22-of-3d-artists-regularly-use-ai)
- CGTrader (Jun 2025–May 2026): AI-generated models are about 1/6 of uploads but about 1/90 of revenue. — [404 Media](https://404media.co/ai-generated-3d-models-flood-market-but-almost-no-one-is-buying-them)
- Motion design: routine 2D motion work is "getting cheaper" due to AI. AI-fluent designers command a 15–30% (or 15–50%) premium; the source is internally inconsistent. Motion Recruitment reports AI-specialization roles up 49% year-on-year, while entry-level and generalist hiring slowed. — [School of Motion](https://schoolofmotion.com/blog/motion-design-salaries-in-2026-your-complete-guide-to-what-you-can-earn-and-how-to-earn-more)
- Commercial AI 3D tools (Meshy, Tripo) are positioned for block-outs, background props and set dressing. They are not yet suited to hero characters, close-up faces or production rigs; these assessments come from vendor and blog sources. — [levelup.gitconnected](https://levelup.gitconnected.com/building-game-ready-3d-assets-fast-the-best-ai-generators-for-production-workflows-in-2026-f1f725ff5df0); [3daistudio comparison](https://www.3daistudio.com/blog/hitem3d-vs-meshy-vs-tripo-comparison)

### Inferences
- **Main driver of the 2023–2025 contraction [synthesis]:** it was mostly cyclical and structural, not AI. Post-pandemic over-hiring in games, the 2023 WGA/SAG-AFTRA strikes, streaming cutbacks and studio mergers, and the Technicolor insolvency are the drivers. No source establishes a causal AI–layoff link. GDC commentary explicitly notes none exists.
- **AI's impact so far shows up in three ways [synthesis]:**
  - Suppressed junior hiring.
  - Cheaper commodity work (simple props, 2D motion, concept iteration).
  - Strong negative sentiment.
  - Direct replacement of 3D production artists is not yet visible in marketplace revenue or artist usage data (CGTrader 1.1%; Poliigon 68% never use AI 3D tools).
- **Exposure ranking for the next 2–3 years [synthesis based on TAG/Future Unscripted role list plus tool capability reviews]:**
  - **Highest:** entry-level and outsourced asset modeling (background props, set dressing), simple product CGI and e-commerce renders, 2D/3D motion templates, roto/paint in VFX.
  - **Medium:** texture/look-dev and environment set dressing, as AI texturing and layout assist matures; animation in-betweening and cleanup.
  - **Lowest:** technical artists, riggers, FX TDs (Houdini), pipeline TDs, real-time/Unreal specialists and senior character artists. These roles integrate AI tools and fix their output, and recruiter and survey sources describe mid/senior and technical shortages, e.g., ScreenSkills mid-to-senior gaps.
- **Regions [synthesis]:**
  - UK, Canada (Vancouver/Montreal) and India service hubs are most exposed to Hollywood volume swings.
  - Japan has a structural labor shortage in anime (rising employment share, falling hours, wage increases).
  - Korea's game workforce still grew (+3.1% in 2024), so the contraction is concentrated in North America and Europe.

### Gaps
- No confirmed final count of Technicolor job losses, or post-2025 status of MPC and The Mill, from primary sources.
- No Animation Guild employment or unemployment-rate data for 2024–2026 was retrieved.
- No independent measurement of how many 3D production jobs have actually been displaced by AI as of 2026; only forecasts (Future Unscripted) and proxy data.
- No layoff breakdown by discipline (e.g., share of laid-off staff who were artists) in GDC 2026.
- Canada: no official VFX/animation employment change for 2023–2025; only production-volume data and an unverified recruiter estimate.
