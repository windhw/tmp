# Industries Outside Mainland China Using 3D DCC Software and Real-Time Engines (Blender, Maya, 3ds Max, Cinema 4D, Houdini, ZBrush, Substance, Unreal, Unity): Uses, Tool Dominance, Market Size, Regional Particularities (as of Oct 2026)

> Scope note: covers North America, Europe, Japan, South Korea, India, Canada, Australia/NZ. Mainland China is covered by another researcher. Market-size figures from Grand View / Technavio / ResearchAndMarkets / IMARC / Marketintelo etc. are **vendor (paid-report) estimates** with inconsistent scopes; they are labelled as such. The WebSearch budget for this session ran out partway through, so several items could not be verified and are listed under Gaps.

## Q1. Which industries outside China use these 3D tools, how do they use them, and which tools dominate (core daily tool vs. occasional use)?

### Takeaway
Ten or so industries use these tools. For four of them (film/TV VFX, animation, games, and archviz plus motion graphics) the tools are core daily production software. Each has a fairly stable "default stack": Maya for animation and characters, Houdini for FX and simulation, 3ds Max with V-Ray/Corona for archviz, Cinema 4D with Redshift for motion graphics, Substance/ZBrush for texturing and sculpting, and Unreal/Unity as engines. Blender is the general-purpose tool that is growing fastest, especially among indies, small studios and freelancers, and now inside some major studios too (Netflix/Animal Logic, Khara). Real-time engines are spreading beyond games into virtual production, automotive HMIs, XR and archviz. Product/e-commerce rendering, medical visualization, fashion, 3D printing and digital twins are smaller or adjacent markets where DCC tools are either specialist niches or one tool among many CAD/PLM tools.

### Cited Findings

#### 1.1 Film/TV visual effects (VFX): core daily tool
- **Size of the global VFX and animation workforce (2025 Atlas):** about 120,000 professionals at 2,450 studios, tracked quarterly. The largest cities were Los Angeles (12,354), Mumbai (10,866) and London (10,564). The workforce grew 9.3% in H2 2024, fell 7.6% in H1 2025, and was up a net 1% over 12 months. The Atlas is sponsored by the British Film Commission and UK Screen Alliance. — [TVBEurope, Jul 2025](https://www.tvbeurope.com/production-post/the-vfx-and-animation-world-atlas-reveals-global-industry-growth-despite-recent-closures)
- **2026 Atlas (Joseph Bell):** more than 140,000 professionals at about 2,900 companies, with net growth of 2.7% over 12 months (up from 1% the year before). LA contracted 1.5% but remains the largest combined VFX and animation hub. Vancouver rebounded by more than 10% (one repost says 11.3%). Ireland is the fastest-growing Western market. The release summary says the data shows "no evidence that AI has cut jobs." Sources disagree on the release date (Aug 24 vs Aug 25, 2026). — [Creative COW](https://creativecow.net/?p=2478405); [CGWORLD flash news](https://cgworld.jp/flashnews/01-202609-VFXatlas.html); [AWN on 2025 edition](https://awn.com/news/joseph-bell-releases-all-new-2025-visual-effects-animation-world-atlas)
- **2025 shock:** the Technicolor group collapsed. MPC and The Mill shut in February 2025 and Jellyfish Pictures closed in March, costing about 500 UK jobs — [TVBEurope](https://www.tvbeurope.com/production-post/the-vfx-and-animation-world-atlas-reveals-global-industry-growth-despite-recent-closures). About 2,000 VFX and animation workers in India lost their jobs in the same collapse — [VFX Voice, Spring 2026](https://vfxvoice.com/the-indian-vfx-industry-a-major-global-force/)
- **Houdini:** SideFX describes Houdini as a major part of feature VFX. It says the Academy has recognized its procedural technology several times, including a Scientific and Technical Award of Merit in 2019, and that many Oscar-winning films relied on it (vendor claim). — [SideFX About](https://download.sidefx.com/company/about-sidefx/)
- **Flagship example: *Dune: Part Two*** won the VFX Oscar at the 97th Academy Awards. Lead vendor DNEG delivered close to 1,000 shots, including sand and pyro simulations, large CG environments and creatures. — [DNEG](https://www.dneg.com/news/dune-part-two-wins-the-oscar-for-visual-effects) (DNEG's page does not name the software. See Inferences.)
- **Typical tools in Indian VFX pipelines, which serve Hollywood:** RenderMan XPU, Houdini, USD and Katana, plus cloud render farms. Work types are roto, paint, matchmove, compositing, asset/CG development and simulation. — [VFX Voice](https://vfxvoice.com/the-indian-vfx-industry-a-major-global-force/)
- **UK VFX economic value (older data):** about £1.043bn a year, with 8,140 direct jobs and 17,490 jobs across the extended value chain (BFI/Olsberg SPI "Screen Business", 2016 data) — [Broadcast](https://broadcastnow.co.uk/tech/vfx-industry-generates-1bn-for-uk-economy/5133387.article). UK Screen Alliance gives 10,680 FTE workers, 5,470 of them on tax-relief work, and £517.6m in direct employment costs — [UK Screen Alliance](https://www.ukscreenalliance.co.uk/?p=1137)

- **Netflix Animation Studios:** this production arm includes the former Animal Logic (acquired 2022). It uses Blender in previs and in its art and matte-painting departments. In January 2026 it joined the Blender Development Fund at the top Corporate Patron tier, at €240,000 or more per year. — [CG Channel, Jan 2026](https://www.cgchannel.com/2026/01/netflix-donates-e240000year-to-the-blender-development-fund/)
- **Other Blender features and series:** *Next Gen* (Netflix, 2018) was made by Tangent Animation (Canada) in Blender — [Lester Banks, Sep 2018](https://lesterbanks.com/?p=52361). - **US animation employment shift (TAG / CVL Economics):** from 2019 to 2024, animation jobs fell 5% in California while they rose 72% in British Columbia (helped by Disney's new Vancouver studio), 18% in New York and about 13% in Ontario. In a survey of 648 Guild members, respondents reported shows such as *SpongeBob*, *Fairly OddParents* and *Looney Tunes* outsourcing parts of their pipelines overseas. — [The Hollywood Reporter](https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/business/business-news/california-animation-work-suffering-outsourcing-concerns-1236206301/)

#### 1.5 Architectural visualization (archviz) and interior design: core daily tool for viz specialists
- **CGarchitect 2021 rendering survey** (2,526 responses, Aug–Dec 2021): V-Ray was used in production by 60.6% (down 4.3 pts) and Corona by 37.2%. Unreal/Twinmotion and the new GPU renderer D5 showed the biggest gains, and Blender's EEVEE entered the top 10. — [CG Channel](https://www.cgchannel.com/?p=133417). In the 2020 survey, 19.6% used Unreal in production and 43.3% were experimenting with it. — [CG Channel 2020](https://www.cgchannel.com/2020/09/cgarchitect-2020-rendering-survey-reveals-trends-in-arch-viz)
- **Modeling tool for archviz (RebusFarm community poll, 886 votes in 2022 and 1,069 in 2024):** 3ds Max held about 58–59% in both years. Blender rose from 4% to 8% and SketchUp fell from 16% to 13%. — [RebusFarm blog](https://rebusfarm.net/blog/the-state-of-3d-modeling-rendering-and-post-production-tools-insights-from-the-archviz-community)
- **Renderer trend 2016–2025 (7CGI compilation, sampling method unstated):** V-Ray fell from 55.9% to 28%, Corona reached 30% and Unreal rose from 0% to 14%. — [7CGI](https://7cgi.com/blog/future-of-3d-visualization/)

### Inferences
- **Core vs. occasional matrix**, based on the findings above plus well-known studio practice. The Blender points are sourced; dominance claims are qualitative where no share data exists.

| Industry | Status of DCC/engine | Dominant tools (outside China) | Blender's position |
|---|---|---|---|
| Film/TV VFX | Core, daily | Maya (anim/layout), Houdini (FX/sim), Nuke, Katana/RenderMan, USD | Niche; used in previs/matte painting at Netflix/Animal Logic; more in small vendors |
| Feature/series animation | Core, daily | Maya (characters), Houdini, Unreal for previs/layout (Imageworks) | Rising: *Flow* (Oscar), *Next Gen*, SPA, Netflix Animation |
| Japanese anime 3DCG | Core in CG departments | 3ds Max/Maya historically; Blender growing | Khara, Project Studio Q (Blender-only) |
| Games (AAA + indie) | Core, daily | Unreal/Unity (+Godot for indies); Maya/3ds Max/Blender for modeling and animation; ZBrush, Substance | Strong among indies; corporate members include Arrowhead, Coffee Stain, Behaviour |
| Motion graphics/broadcast/advertising | Core for motion designers | Cinema 4D + Redshift + After Effects; Unreal for real-time broadcast | Growing among freelancers/YouTubers (no hard data) |
| Archviz/interiors | Core for viz specialists; occasional for architects | 3ds Max + V-Ray/Corona; SketchUp; Unreal/Twinmotion, D5, Enscape | ~8% modeling share in RebusFarm poll; EEVEE in top 10 renderers |
| Product viz/e-commerce | Core for render studios; occasional for brands | 3ds Max/C4D/Blender + KeyShot/V-Ray; CAD upstream | Common among freelancers |
| XR apps | Core (engine) | Unity (majority of Quest titles per Unity), Unreal | Asset creation |
| Virtual production | Core on LED stages | Unreal Engine (StageCraft etc.) | Asset creation |
| Automotive HMI/configurators/digital twin | Core for viz/HMI teams; CAD dominates engineering | Unreal; Autodesk VRED/Alias (not researched here) | BMW is a Blender fund member |
| Medical/scientific, fashion, 3D printing, education | Specialist or occasional | Maya/C4D/Blender (medical); CLO3D/Marvelous/Browzwear (fashion); slicers/CAD (3D printing) | Strong in education and 3D-printing hobbyists (no hard data) |

- In established studios, Autodesk tools (Maya, 3ds Max) still anchor the large-studio pipeline in film, animation and archviz. But Autodesk's M&E revenue is small and growing slowly (see Q2), while Blender's corporate backing grew sharply in 2025–26 (Netflix, NVIDIA, Qualcomm, Wacom, PICO, BMW, Microsoft, AMD, Intel). That suggests the large studios are hedging toward Blender rather than replacing their pipelines.
- Real-time engines are the most important cross-industry shift. Unreal is now used in previs/layout for big animated features (Imageworks), in LED virtual production, in automotive HMIs and in archviz (19.6% in 2020, rising). This widens the market for engine-trained 3D artists beyond games.
- *Dune: Part Two*'s sand and pyro simulations are textbook Houdini work, and Houdini is standard at DNEG, but none of the sources read here explicitly name Houdini for that film. Treat that link as inference.

### Gaps
- There is no independent, current (2024–26) market-share survey of DCC tools by industry. CGarchitect's last survey found here is from 2021, Poliigon's software-by-industry chart is skewed toward hobbyists, and the Blender survey charts were not readable as text.
- No market-size data was found for motion graphics, broadcast design or advertising CGI specifically. Maxon/Nemetschek segment revenue could not be retrieved because the search budget ran out.
- No hard data was found on Blender's share in motion graphics, product viz, medical viz or 3D printing.
- No official Epic count of Unreal film/TV or virtual production projects was found. The LED stage counts are unverified.
- No SideFX revenue or user numbers; SideFX is private.

## Q2. How big are the global markets (3D animation / VFX / games / 3D rendering) in 2024–2026, and what do vendor revenues indicate?

### Takeaway
Games are by far the largest market that uses these tools: about $201.6bn in consumer spending in 2025 (Newzoo). The anime "market" (Japan, consumer-side) was ¥3.84tn (~$25bn) in 2024. Pure VFX services are estimated at roughly $10–15bn in 2025 (vendor estimates). The 3D rendering/visualization software and services market is estimated at roughly $4–9bn, with archviz the largest application. The software vendors themselves are small by comparison. Autodesk M&E (Maya, 3ds Max) was $332M in FY2026, Unity's total revenue $1.85bn (mostly ads), and the Blender Foundation's income about €3.1M (2024). Market-research figures disagree by orders of magnitude depending on scope, so they should be used only with their definitions stated.

### Cited Findings
- **Games (end-user spending):** $201.6bn in 2025 (up 9.1%), forecast $234.4bn in 2028 (Newzoo) — [GamesBeat](https://gamesbeat.com/global-games-revenue-breached-200b-in-2025-newzoo/). KOCCA's estimate of the 2024 global game market is $220.71bn, using a different method — [Asia Economy](https://view.asiae.co.kr/en/article/2026032515223006138)
- **VFX (vendor estimates):** $9.65bn in 2024, $10.83bn in 2025 and $25.15bn by 2032 at a 12.7% CAGR (ResearchAndMarkets) — [Business Wire, Dec 2025](https://secure.businesswire.com/news/home/20251224406581/en/The-Visual-Effects-Market-Global-Industry-Dynamics-and-Strategic-Forecast-Report-2025-2032-Featuring-3DAR-Adobe-Digital-Domain-Eastman-Kodak-Weta-FX-Zoic-Studios-and-More---ResearchAndMarkets.com). ### Inferences
- The value chain is inverted. End markets (games about $200bn, anime about $25bn, VFX about $10–15bn) are 100–1000 times larger than the software vendors' DCC revenue (Autodesk M&E $0.33bn). Most of the money in these industries goes to labor (artists), not tools, which is why the "industry size" most relevant to 3D careers is workforce headcount (about 140,000 VFX and animation professionals globally in the 2026 Atlas, plus games employment).
- Autodesk M&E growth (5%) trailing its AEC/MFG growth (16–22%) is consistent with competitive pressure from Blender, Unreal and Houdini and with the 2023–25 entertainment slowdown. This is an inference; Autodesk does not attribute it.
- Blender's Development Fund now runs at roughly $3.9M a year ($326,743 × 12). That is small next to commercial vendors, but it funds a tool with a very large free user base.

## Q3. Notable examples: Blender in professional production, and Maya/Houdini/Unreal flagship productions

### Takeaway
2025–26 was a breakout period for Blender's professional legitimacy. *Flow* (made in Blender, rendered in EEVEE) won the 2025 Oscar for Best Animated Feature. Netflix Animation Studios (top tier), NVIDIA, Qualcomm, Wacom, PICO, BMW, Microsoft, AMD, Intel, Adobe and Chaos now fund Blender, and Japanese studio Khara completed its move to Blender. Maya, Houdini and Unreal remain the backbone of blockbuster work: *KPop Demon Hunters* (Maya + Unreal + USD), *Dune: Part Two* (DNEG, simulation-heavy) and *The Mandalorian* (Unreal on ILM StageCraft).

## Q4. Regional particularities (Japan, South Korea, India, Canada, UK/Europe, US, Australia/NZ)

- **US (LA):** the largest hub, but contracting.
- **India (Mumbai):** now the second-largest VFX and animation city by headcount. It is an outsourcing-driven industry, with 85–90% of revenue from foreign clients.
- **London:** third-largest, and the leading hub in Europe.
- **Canada (Vancouver, Montreal, Toronto):** has gained the most from tax credits, with BC animation jobs up 72% from 2019 to 2024.

### Inferences
- Tax incentives largely decide where VFX and animation work goes: Canada at 27–41% in Québec, the UK uplift, India's 40% incentive, Australia's PDV. - Blender's adoption pattern differs by region. In Europe it has grown from a grassroots base (most survey respondents, Amsterdam foundation, *Flow*). In Japan it spreads top-down through Khara and its satellite studios, driven by license cost and outsourcing compatibility. In India it is a large user base (second in the survey) that sits alongside Maya/Houdini-based outsourcing pipelines.
- Korea's 3D use is spread more across games, webtoons (3D backgrounds) and fashion (CLO) than across film VFX.
